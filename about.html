ABOUT THE EDUCATOR

Technology with a human touch.

I am an educator working to make vocational and digital learning practical, understandable and meaningful for students. My approach combines classroom learning with hands-on activities so that students can connect concepts with real-life situations.

Through IT–ITeS education, the focus is on digital confidence, employability skills, communication, problem solving and responsible use of technology.
